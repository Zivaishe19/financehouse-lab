Investigation checklist

How to test the story

Ask what evidence supports each claim, rather than relying on the interface alone.

01
Trace deposits

Are deposits independently recorded? Can wallet addresses and transaction hashes be verified on-chain?

02
Verify trading

Do exchange statements show actual executions, fees, positions and realised profit?

03
Trace payouts

Can each withdrawal be reconciled with bank, payment-provider or blockchain records?

04
Test sustainability

Can payouts be funded by independently verified profits, without depending on new deposits?
